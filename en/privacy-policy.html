Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026 · In effect under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA)

CS.SIGN And Products Co., Ltd. ("the Company", "we") takes your privacy and personal data seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal data when you use the website cssign.co.th or contact us through our various channels, in accordance with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562.

1. Personal Data We Collect

When you fill out a quotation request form, a contact form, download our catalog, or use the chatbot on our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Your full name and company/organization name
  • Phone number and email address
  • Any message or details you provide, such as products of interest or project details
  • Technical data such as IP address and device/browser type, collected automatically via cookies for website analytics

2. Purposes of Collection and Use

  • To respond to your inquiry, provide consultation, and prepare a quotation as requested
  • To fulfil a sale or service agreement, including delivery and installation
  • To improve the quality of our website and services
  • To comply with the law or with orders from an authorized government agency

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, where you have filled out a form on the website; on the basis of contract, where a sale or service is provided; and on the basis of legitimate interest, in improving and maintaining the security of the website.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. We may disclose data to service providers who work with us, such as email service providers, cloud storage providers (e.g. Firebase/Google Cloud), and logistics providers, only to the extent necessary to provide our services to you. Such providers are required to keep your data confidential to an appropriate standard, and we will disclose data only as required by law or by order of an authorized government agency.

5. Data Retention Period

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes stated in this policy, or as required by law. For example, data submitted through a contact form is retained for no longer than 2 years from your last contact, unless there is an ongoing contractual relationship or a legal/tax requirement to retain it for longer.

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to remember your preferences and to analyze overall website usage. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; however, disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

7. Data Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and administrative measures to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of personal data, such as encrypting data in transit (HTTPS) and restricting data access to relevant staff only.

8. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the PDPA, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

RightDescription
Right to accessRequest to know about and obtain a copy of the personal data we hold
Right to rectificationRequest that inaccurate or outdated data be corrected
Right to erasureRequest that data be deleted or destroyed once no longer needed
Right to withdraw consentWithdraw previously given consent at any time
Right to objectObject to the collection, use, or disclosure of data in certain cases

You may exercise these rights by contacting us through the channels below. We will act on your request within the timeframe required by law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in law or in our business practices. The date of the most recent update will be indicated at the top of this page.
