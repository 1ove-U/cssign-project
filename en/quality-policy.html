Product Warranty

Safety signs manufactured by CS.SIGN carry an 18-month warranty on materials and workmanship, from the date of delivery or completed installation. Other product groups carry a 6-month warranty. If a manufacturing defect is found within the warranty period, our team is glad to inspect and repair it at no charge.

Covered

Defects in material or workmanship, such as peeling or flaking paint, or a warped structure resulting from substandard manufacturing.

Not Covered

Damage from accidents, misuse, natural disasters, modification by others, or normal wear from long-term outdoor use.

Need to file a warranty claim? Have your order number and photos of the damage ready, then contact our team right away.

MANUFACTURING STANDARDS

Standards We Manufacture To

Every sign is designed and manufactured to the standards below. If you need supporting documentation for procurement, please contact our team.

TIS

Thai Industrial Standard TIS

Safety signs are manufactured to the shape, colour, and symbol requirements of Thai Industrial Standard TIS.

DOH
STD

Department of Highways Standard — Traffic Signs

All traffic signs are manufactured to Department of Highways requirements for materials, colour, size, and reflective film.
